The big idea

Beauty is the whole point of this route — so the plan spends the budget where it matters and saves everywhere else. Gas is the biggest and most fixable cost: buy it cheap in Kansas, Colorado, and Utah, then coast into California on near-empty. Every hotel is already booked at a free or fixed rate.

Before you leave

The car's in good shape — the one real gap is no spare tire, so the plan below makes the tires and a plug kit your safety net.

Car — status

  • Engine oil — just changed, good ~6,000 mi
  • Coolant — full (Zerex Asian Green, MS 591-08)
  • Brakes — good (still gear down on long descents)
  • Wiper fluid — full · chargers packed
  • No spare tire — no room in the Soul; see plan →
  • Inspect all 4 tires: tread (penny test), even wear, sidewall cracks/bulges, pressure to door-jamb spec

No-spare plan

  • Tire plug kit + 12V compressor (~$25–35, Walmart/AutoZone) — fixes the common nail puncture roadside in ~15 min
  • Confirm roadside assistance: auto insurance, Discover card benefits, any leftover Kia roadside
  • A sidewall blowout can't be plugged — that's the tow case, and a UT/NV desert tow is long and pricey
  • A can of Fix-a-Flat is cheap backup insurance

Documents — glovebox folder

  • Temp registration tag — valid through July 9, covers the trip
  • Bill of sale, proof of insurance, Kansas license
  • Printed/offline copies of all 4 hotel confirmations

Pack

  • Cooler + groceries + a case of water (covers the empty desert stretches)
  • Sunglasses (salt-flat glare), light jacket (passes are cool even in June)
  • Offline Google Maps for Utah and Nevada — long dead-zones
  • First-aid kit, paper towels, trash bags, power bank

Safety & driving notes

  • Day 1 is the long one — swap drivers every ~2 hours, stop when drowsy. No heroics.
  • Mountain passes (Eisenhower, Vail, Donner) are clear and easy in June, but use a lower gear on long descents to save the brakes.
  • Desert dead-zones — keep the tank above ¼, carry water, download offline maps for UT/NV. Signal disappears for long stretches.
  • After casino nights — never take the morning wheel if you've been drinking. Comped drinks add up fast.
  • Salt-flat glare — sunglasses on Day 3; the white plain is blinding.
