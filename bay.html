Bay Area day trips

Ten days to explore from one of the best-placed launch pads in California. Tap any pin for drive time and one-tap directions. Distances are from your Fremont base.

San Francisco

~50 min by BART · skip the car

Take BART from Warm Springs / South Fremont downtown — no bridge tolls, no parking. Ferry Building, Golden Gate Park, the Mission, the bridge. Go on a clear day; summer fog burns off by midday.

Monterey & Big Sur

Monterey ~1.5 hr · Big Sur beyond

Monterey Bay Aquarium and Cannery Row, then 17-Mile Drive and CA-1 south into Big Sur — Bixby Bridge, McWay Falls. Check CA-1 is open before going deep.

Directions ↗

Yosemite Valley

~3.5–4 hr via CA-120

Waterfalls still strong in June. A reservation may be required to enter midday in summer — check nps.gov first. Treat it as a full day or an overnight.

Directions ↗

Lake Tahoe

~3.5 hr · alpine blue

Emerald Bay, alpine swims, a full-blue June lake. Long day or an overnight; pairs naturally with a Sierra loop.

Santa Cruz

~50 min over CA-17

Beach, boardwalk, and redwoods nearby — the easy coastal half-day.

Mission Peak

In Fremont · sunrise hike

The 2,517-ft landmark over your base. ~3.3 mi up, ~2,150 ft gain, no shade — go at sunrise, bring water. Park at Ohlone College to dodge the Stanford Ave permit scramble.
